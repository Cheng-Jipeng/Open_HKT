# V6 parameter routes and measurement: independent reconciliation

Prepared 29 September 2026. Read-only assessment of the current parameter-groups table, measurability audit, external-literature review and supplied deep-research report. No data download, parameter estimation, equilibrium solve or model edit was performed. Additional equation checks use the active V6 manuscript; saved empirical diagnostics are read as dated evidence, not refreshed access tests.

## Main judgment

Keep A/B/C as an allocation of calibration work, not as an identification classification. A directly measured aggregate may discipline a structurally latent parameter through a conditional inversion; conversely, an equation containing a parameter does not establish its identification. There are useful paths that eliminate the absolute knowledge stock. Their limiting requirement is an accurate observation rule for production, capitalization and innovation financing, not an observation of every model symbol.

Use the source-verified TeX review as the numerical evidence ledger. The deep report supplies alternative judgments and scenarios, not a second independent sample of evidence. Neither report delivers a uniquely defensible external vector. In particular, choice of the input margin for rho, the cohort period for beta, and the financial/operating-asset perimeter must precede final assignment.

Status language below: **external candidate** means an independently estimated but imperfectly transferable coefficient; **conditional moment route** means an algebraically valid structural fit whose observation rule and identification still require verification; **scenario** means maintained uncertainty; **data input** means a scoped empirical quantity; **normalization/fixed** means no separate estimation claim.

## Valid N-free routes and what their data must mean

### Production inversion and conditional labor decomposition

Write W_H=w_H H and W_L=w_L L, including research workers in H. V6 implies

\[
Y-W_L=P X=\frac{w_H\varphi H}{\vartheta},\qquad
\mathcal D=\frac{1-\vartheta}{\vartheta}w_H\varphi H.
\]

Hence, conditional on measured phi, a production-moment route is
\[
\vartheta=\frac{w_H\varphi H}{Y-W_L}
=\frac{w_H\varphi H}{\mathcal D+w_H\varphi H}.
\]
These two expressions are not independent moments when D is itself the output-account residual. Alternatively, given an external vartheta, infer phi=vartheta(Y−W_L)/W_H. Check 0<phi≤1 instead of truncating infeasible values.

If matched aggregate compensation e, skill premium r=w_H/w_L and labor quantities are available, conditional levels satisfy
\[
w_L=\frac{e}{rH+L},\qquad w_H=\frac{re}{rH+L}.
\]
This can support a RoW route despite missing wage levels, but it is compensation-scaled structural measurement (I), not new directly observed skill compensation. Earnings ratios, employer benefits, ages, sectors and countries must align.

With X=phi H, s_X=(Y−W_L)/Y and rho≠1,
\[
A_X=\frac YX(s_X/\alpha)^{1/(1-\rho)},\qquad
A_L=\frac YL[(1-s_X)/(1-\alpha)]^{1/(1-\rho)}.
\]
At N_0=1 these give the initial u bars. No observed absolute N is required. They do not separately identify rho, alpha, phi and all scale coefficients from one observation. Recovered quantities use the same output/share data; counting every recovered object as an additional calibration moment exaggerates information.

### Capitalization/free-entry route for a and knowledge growth

Let Q denote V6 post-issuance capitalization, I denote the same date's entrant financing and q the common ex-dividend incumbent/entrant price. From Q=N_{t+1}q and I=(N_{t+1}−N_t)q,
\[
Q-I=N_tq,\qquad G_N=\frac Q{Q-I}.
\]
Under active R&D, aN_tq=w_H and I=(1−phi)Hw_H. Therefore
\[
a=\frac{w_H}{Q-I},\qquad
aH=\frac{W_H}{Q-I}
=\left[\frac Q{W_H}-(1-\varphi)\right]^{-1}.
\]
These are exact conditional identities, algebraically verified with a synthetic interior observation; that verification is not an empirical test. They require 0<I<Q and a common period/currency/claim universe. With zero research, the law alone gives G_N=1 and does not identify a. The free-entry **inequality** adds a≤w_H/Q, not equality, if Q and w_H are validly mapped.

The empirical bottlenecks are substantive:

- **Q:** AHP enterprise value is a directly constructed operating-asset value under financing neutrality. It is a legitimate candidate, but using it as V6 post-issuance equity capitalization requires consistent claims and the same payout model. A listed-equity capitalization alternative has different coverage. Subtracting equity-only external positions from EV is conditional on the declared operating-asset/equity convention.
- **I:** V6 I is new-variety finance paid to skilled research workers. Gross capital formation includes physical and other intangible investment; total R&D spending includes nonlabor inputs; net equity issuance includes repurchases and other corporate transactions. None is automatically I. Research payroll constructed from matched R and w_H is a conditional route, provided R is genuinely a subset of the same H and entry/free-entry assumptions are maintained.
- **Timing:** Q must be after current issuance and I the value of those new claims at the same ex-dividend price. Using the same empirical N for both N_t and N_{t+1} silently deletes the growth adjustment. The existing annual implied-a diagnostic correctly distinguishes beginning and end-year stock timing.
- **Circularity:** If I is constructed as Q−w_H/a for a chosen a, recovering a from this expression is a tautology. Likewise, knowledge generated using an assumed a cannot independently validate a. If independently measured Q and research payroll imply a_t, temporal constancy and genuinely held-out knowledge growth can be tested, but this becomes a production/valuation calibration, not a literature assignment.

### Foreign-yield route without full RoW capitalization

For active research, the V6 per-claim yield satisfies
\[
\frac dq=aH\frac{1-\vartheta}{\vartheta}\varphi,
\qquad
aH=\frac dq\frac{\vartheta}{(1-\vartheta)\varphi}.
\]
AHP constructs foreign monetary yield C^A_t/(E_{A,t−1}+G^A_t), avoiding full V_W. This can discipline a_WH_W under the homogeneous-claim/payout bridge. It does not require absolute N or q. However, monetary FDI dividends, reinvested earnings, portfolio distributions, taxes/repatriation and currency valuation need a matched basket. This empirical yield is not automatically V6 incumbent profit payout. Aggregate D/Q instead equals (d/q)/G_N; substituting it unadjusted changes the equation.

### Growth elasticities without an independently observed N level

Within u, with constant scale coefficients and recovered augmentations,
\[
\log(A_{X,t+1}/A_{X,t})=\xi_u\log G_{N,t},\quad
\log(A_{L,t+1}/A_{L,t})=\nu_u\log G_{N,t}.
\]
One may use the independently mapped Q/I ratio for G_N rather than a patent stock. This yields model-conditional estimating restrictions, not directly observed technology elasticities. Measurement error is shared across production and financing variables; constant-a and constant-exponent restrictions must be assessed jointly. G_N close to one produces weak ratios. At G_N=1 both exponents disappear from these growth equations. A regime switch also changes the scales and invalidates an unadjusted within-u ratio.

## Saving, international ownership and the single-safe-bond bridge

In the V6 asset universe, equity clearing and the US NFA definition give
\[
A=Q_{US}+NFA,\quad A^*=Q_W-NFA,\quad
\beta=A/e_{US},\quad
\chi=\frac{A^*/e_W-\beta}{1-A^*/e_W}.
\]
These are conditional identities for cohort post-trade saving, not national wealth identities under arbitrary data substitution. The saved 22 September diagnostic uses end-year US corporate EV plus all-sector NFA divided by annual corporate compensation; all 36 observations in 1990–2025 exceed one. Its own note identifies multiple-cohort wealth, corporate-versus-national scope and annual-versus-generational mismatch. It is a failed mapping diagnostic, not evidence for beta>1. Annual net saving is a flow across ages and differs again. Changing period length requires rebuilding cohort income, ownership and flows, not dividing by an arbitrary number of years.

The measurable external primitives are E_A,E_L,NFA and their matched flows/revaluations. Under an explicit common equity universe,
\[
\omega=\frac{Q_{US}-E_L}{Q_{US}-E_L+E_A},\qquad
\omega^*=\frac{E_L}{Q_W-E_A+E_L}.
\]
The first ratio has a feasible US construction; the second still needs Q_W. AHP's E_L/Q_US is issuer ownership, not omega-star and not a preference center. An imputed Q_W obtained from an assumed foreign portfolio weight cannot independently identify that weight or its center.

The net non-equity stock b_NE=NFA−E_A+E_L and flow F_NE=CA−(P_A−P_L) are direct accounting residuals. They need not satisfy F_NE=Delta b_NE because revaluation and other changes intervene. Broad non-equity assets include more than a single safe claim. To map them into b, specify netting, maturities, currencies, credit risk, residence and the zero-net-supply model bond. Foreign Treasuries provide a more specific numerator but not the complete net position or investable-wealth denominator. A residual with a sign incompatible with the model's positive foreign US-bond demand must be reported, not sign-flipped.

There is a useful exact chi identity from V6 bond pricing. Define
\[
x=\left(1-\frac{R_f}{R_f^W}\right)\frac{b^*_{US}}{e_W}
=\frac{\chi}{1+\chi},\qquad \chi=\frac{x}{1-x}.
\]
It uses gross returns and b*=B*/R_f, not an arbitrary spread-times-face-value formula. Crucially, R_f^W is the zero-supply shadow RoW safe return; it is model implied in the current measurement audit. Thus this is not an independently observable shortcut for chi. A usable empirical spread must separately justify identical currency, maturity, payoff risk and convenience services.

For kappa/centers/eta, realized returns are not conditional expectations or pricing kernels. AHP NFA, CA and VA additionally obey accounting identities. Residual closure cannot be added as independent evidence. Check moment-Jacobian rank, weak directions and profiles conditional on A/B; if the five-dimensional C block is not distinguishable, report combinations or impose explicit restrictions. Upsilon'(theta)=1/(1−theta)+theta vanishes at theta=(1−sqrt(5))/2: that FOC alone loses eta information there. Boundary equity positions require inequalities rather than the interior equalities.

## Reconcile the two reviews without manufacturing consensus

1. **Gamma:** the deep report's center near one and 0.5–4 scenario envelope differ from the TeX review's finance-oriented five, lifecycle alternative two and much higher recursive-utility evidence. These are different judgments over heterogeneous evidence, not competing point estimates of the same V6 object. Preserve both conceptual branches; the 0.5–4 envelope cannot be called coverage of the HRS/recursive evidence summarized in the TeX ledger.
2. **Markups:** deep-report mu=1.4 is a chosen midpoint of dated US estimates, not an original estimate or a representative-firm correction. Prefer the TeX ledger's separately labeled cost-definition and regional candidates; equal US/RoW markups remain an explicit pooling scenario.
3. **Rho:** the deep report promotes the capital–labor branch partly as operational under the current solver. The TeX review prioritizes a skill-IV candidate and newer skill meta-evidence while retaining complementarity as an analogy. Do not resolve this by solver feasibility or average the branches. State the empirical input mapping before choosing a branch.
4. **Pi:** the deep report gives annual pi=.96 for a 25-year geometric duration, then five-year pi=.80 by redefining geometric duration in coarse periods. That is a new discretized process, not exact aggregation of annual survival (.96^5=.81537), or of Kahn–Rich quarterly persistence (.99^20=.81791). Declare the event clock and preserve its survival law; the 10/25/50-year grid is a scenario design.
5. **Absorbing scales:** deep-report bar-A_b/bar-A_u=1 is continuity only at N_dagger=1 (or a zero corresponding exponent). Correct severity ratios are m_X=bar-A_X,b/(bar-A_X,u N_dagger^xi) and m_L=bar-A_L,b/(bar-A_L,u N_dagger^nu). The TeX review already makes this qualification. Its 0.8/1/1.2 and the deep report's 0.75/1/1.25 are alternative analyst grids, not conflicting empirical ranges.
6. **Alpha:** a pure gauge change must preserve both composites in every branch. Outcome differences after changing alpha do not establish its economic identification if b scales or other restrictions were not transformed consistently. The rho=1 exception must remain explicit.
7. **a and xi/nu:** both reviews correctly reject portable literature numbers, but should not imply that an independent exact N series is the only possible discipline. The conditional N-free routes above are richer. They may move entries from 'no viable moment route' to 'conditional route needing an observation rule', never automatically to 'identified'. HKT values and the deep report's exponent box are comparison/scenario values.
8. **Version/provenance:** the TeX review pins HKT arXiv v2 and records original page/table locators. The deep report refers to a later 2026 version and retains opaque ChatGPT citation tokens, without usable source links in the saved file. Its extra claims should be kept as supplied-review claims until independently traced; do not silently substitute that version for the authoritative local definitions.

## Verified supplement: AHP opening-basket monetary yield

Rechecked against the supplied AHP transcription and saved empirical implementation on 29 September 2026. Define
\[
y^{cash}_{W,t}\equiv
\frac{C^A_t}{E_{A,t-1}+VA^A_t},
\]
where C^A_t is monetary dividend receipts on US-held foreign equity during period t, E_{A,t-1} is its opening/end-of-previous-period market value, and VA^A_t is the current-period **positive-US-asset revaluation** (a signed nominal holding gain or loss). VA^A is neither gross knowledge growth nor the total change in the equity position. The denominator adds revaluation to opening holdings, **not** current net acquisitions/financial transactions or other-volume changes. Price and currency revaluation are not automatically separated by this USD construction.

**Exact source locators.** `Codes/Reference/Atkeson_2025_End_of_Privilege.tex`, Appendix A4, journal p.2198: line 1876 identifies monetary receipts and explicitly states that full RoW enterprise value is not constructed; line 1880 defines opening holdings plus current revaluation, referring to IMA S.9 line 100; line 1884 takes the monetary payout/current-price-value ratio. AHP calls the resulting ratio D*_t/V*_t in its own model. That source label is not permission to equate it to V6 aggregate D_t/Q_t.

**Exact V6 correspondence under the matched incumbent-basket assumption.** If the same beginning-of-period holdings n_{W,t-1} generate all three objects,
\[
E_{A,t-1}=n_{W,t-1}q_{W,t-1},\quad
VA^A_t=n_{W,t-1}(q_{W,t}-q_{W,t-1}),\quad
C^A_t=n_{W,t-1}d_{W,t},
\]
then y_cash=d_{W,t}/q_{W,t} for a positive opening basket. Active R&D and V6 `country_dividend_yield` therefore imply
\[
a_WH_W=y^{cash}_{W,t}
\frac{\vartheta_W}{(1-\vartheta_W)\varphi_{W,t}}.
\]
This is a distinct **input route** that avoids both full Q_W and absolute N_W. It remains the same free-entry/dividend restriction, not an additional independent condition when its inputs have been generated from other fitted quantities.

**Mapping status and caveats.** The AHP monetary-yield statistic is an accounting construction; its equality to V6 d/q is **I**, conditional on accurate basket and payout mapping. Monetary distributions omit separately reported reinvested earnings; FDI, portfolio equity and fund coverage must agree between payouts and holdings. Within-period acquisitions and dividend timing can prevent actual receipts or revaluation from representing precisely the opening basket. Taxes/repatriation and currency movements require explicit treatment. Empirical monetary payouts must match V6 incumbent profit distributions, rather than automatically being replaced by AHP FCF or current earnings. Match the model period before using the yield for aH. Finally, V6 aggregate D_t/Q_t=(d_t/q_t)/G_{N,t}; this distinction survives even though the foreign-basket construction cancels the number of claims.
