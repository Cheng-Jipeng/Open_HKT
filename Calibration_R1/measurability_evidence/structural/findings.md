## Recommended decision rule

- **D — directly measured:** a documented statistic measures the model object after an explicitly stated universe and timing convention. Currency conversion, scaling, and simple arithmetic do not by themselves make an observable structurally latent. D need not mean error-free. A retrieved series with a related name is insufficient.
- **I — indirectly measured / model implied:** reconstructable from measured components with a defensible bridge, or an explicitly conditional model reconstruction. State which type applies. Data-based accounting reconstruction and a number generated solely by the model are not equivalent evidence.
- **L — structurally latent:** a preference/technology/regime construct that needs a structural estimating/calibration equation, a maintained restriction, or an operational definition not currently supplied. An inverse equilibrium formula does not establish an accurate measurement mapping if its arguments are themselves unmapped.

Keep **mapping status** separate from **access status**. A working API does not establish the mapping; a failed API does not prove the quantity unmeasurable. In particular, do not promote a parameter from L to D because a numerical solver returns it or because an externally estimated coefficient can be downloaded. For the parameter rows below, 'L currently' is the defensible default; possible routes to identification are described separately.

### The distinction between $\beta$ and a national saving rate is substantive

V6 agents live for two periods and the young save labor earnings. Assets purchased from the old are included in young saving even when they do not create national saving. BEA personal saving sums the personal sector and excludes capital gains; its denominator includes nonlabor income and transfers before subtracting taxes. It cannot identify the cohort saving propensity through a renaming of its series. SCF is valuable for cross-sectional household wealth and income, but observed wealth stocks and qualitative spending-versus-income responses also do not automatically deliver a panel measure of the model's period asset acquisition. A separate household/cohort measurement exercise is required.

### Two useful structural relationships (conditional, not observed identities)

1. If genuinely compatible saving propensities $s_{US}=A/e_{US}$ and $s_W=A^*/e_W$ were measured, $\beta=s_{US}$ and $\chi=(s_W-\beta)/(1-s_W)$. Their algebraic recoverability does not cure the current missing cohort/OLG-period mapping.
2. The portfolio centers and cost strength appear together with risk-adjusted return moments. Multiple data series or multiple fitted targets are not a proof of separate identification. An eventual calibration should examine rank/sensitivity and report restricted parameters separately from estimated ones.

## Cross-cutting cautions for the parent tables

- Actual equity weights have observable-position candidates, but aggregate ownership includes old and young households, intermediaries, government, pensions, and institutions; the model's investing cohort bridge remains a decision.
- A quoted nominal safe yield is observable; the model's one-period gross real safe return is usually I after maturity, compounding, numeraire, and expected-inflation mapping. An ex-post deflated Treasury return is not literally an ex-ante risk-free real payoff. The zero-supply RoW bond is especially a model-implied counterfactual unless a matching instrument is justified.
- A normalized knowledge initial condition of one is imposed, not evidence that knowledge has been measured. Normalize separately from labeling it a direct observation.
- L does not mean economically dispensable or unidentifiable in every design. It means the current evidence does not supply an accurate direct mapping; these rows require joint calibration, external structural estimates, or openly stated restrictions.

## Addendum: a useful D category without weakening the mapping standard

Use **two axes**: measurement class D/I/L, and mapping readiness (ready / conditional / gap). D means a reported statistic, microdata aggregation, or transparent accounting aggregate measures an explicitly defined empirical counterpart **without solving the model or imposing its behavioral first-order conditions**. Ratios, currency conversion, deflation, and sums do not automatically make a quantity I. Standard sampling and statistical estimation do not disqualify national-accounts observables from D. D/conditional must explicitly name the unresolved economic mapping; it does not mean calibration-ready.

Do not require literal satisfaction of every model abstraction before any output can be D. At the end-of-period observation point the model's equity holders are the young; national end-of-period holdings can still be the empirical counterpart of aggregate country portfolios because the model's old have liquidated. This requires a declared aggregation interpretation, not necessarily an age-filtered national securities account. By contrast, that aggregation argument does not equate national net saving/DPI with the young cohort's gross asset acquisition/labor income.
